GOOD TO KNOW

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS.

Everything you need to know before your Red Sea escape.

How do I book a private speedboat trip? +
Simply choose your preferred trip, select your date and number of guests, and send us your booking request through WhatsApp. Our team will contact you to confirm the details.
Is the boat private or shared? +
MANTA offers private speedboat experiences. The boat is reserved exclusively for you and your group.
What is included in the trip? +
Each experience has different inclusions. Please check the details of your selected trip or contact our team for more information.
Can we choose our own departure time? +
Yes, you can request your preferred departure time. We will do our best to arrange it depending on availability and weather conditions.
Are hotel transfers available? +
Please contact our team with your hotel location. We will let you know the available transfer options.
What happens if the weather is bad? +
Your safety always comes first. If weather or sea conditions are unsuitable, our team will contact you to discuss rescheduling or other available arrangements.
