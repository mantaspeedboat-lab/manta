Before You Set Sail

A few things to know before enjoying your MANTA experience.

What to Bring

  • Towel and a change of clothes
  • Sunscreen, sunglasses and a hat
  • Phone or camera for the views
  • A little cash for extras or tips

Good to Know

  • Trips depend on weather conditions. If the sea is not safe, we will reschedule your trip.
  • Up to 8 adults can join the private boat.
  • The price is for the whole boat, not per person.
  • Tips for the crew are always appreciated, but never expected.
